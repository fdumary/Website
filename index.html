About Me

I'm Francesca Dumary, a student at Florida Atlantic University pursuing a Master of Science degree in Computer Science. I specialize in software engineering, web development, and graphic design. Previously, I was a Product Development intern for Devin, where I prototyped an "AI Companion" specializing in wellness. I am currently the Administrative Officer for the FAU Chapter of Women in Engineering & Computer Science (WIECS), an organization dedicated to supporting female students at FAU's College of Engineering & Computer Science. I have also participated in hackathons, where I developed software projects and sparked a passion for programming.
